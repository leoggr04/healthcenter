# Surface: index (landing page)

Scope: single long-scroll donation/information page for Förderverein Rose Health Center, plus two subordinate legal pages (Impressum, Datenschutz) and a downloadable fillable membership PDF. Visitor mode: **Persuade**.

Audience: German-speaking donors and prospective Förderverein members, arriving via a shared link on phone or desktop, deciding in a few minutes whether to donate or join.

Job: understand what the Rose Health Center and its school do in Buniadu, Gambia, see evidence it is real and effective, then act — bank transfer/QR donation now, or download the membership form.

Proof/content: real photos (founders, rescue vehicle, staff, community, clinic interior), concrete patient/staff statistics, a named leader (Salieu Tinn), a transparent bank account, real contact emails/phone numbers.

Constraints: all copy is fixed, verbatim, from the client-supplied Figma export (`figma_design_css`); stats and history must not be altered; Impressum/Datenschutz ship with clearly labeled placeholder legal content; membership form is a standalone fillable PDF, not a web form.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Trust through specificity, not appeal through sentiment — replace the generic "help Africa" ask with one exact place, one named vehicle, one named nurse, and real counted numbers, so the visitor sees precisely what their euro buys instead of a mood.

OWN-WORLD: White ground; one committed brand blue (#0560B2) plus a lighter sky accent (#0690DF); soft blue-tinted panels (9,143,222 at 20–40% alpha) as section grounds, never a gray card. Inter throughout — clean, legible at small sizes, reads like a well-run association's own material rather than a startup. Every real photograph rides in a large rounded card (radius ~19–42px) with a soft offset shadow. Actions are pill-shaped solid-blue buttons in white type.

STORY: A donor lands, immediately reads what this specific center does and where (hero), meets its founding story through a real photo gallery including the actual rescue vehicle their donations funded, sees the scale of need in hard counted numbers (clinic stats, rain-season spike), meets the school and the staff through real photographs, then acts — bank transfer or QR code now, or download the form to become a recurring member.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Header bar (logo mark, phone numbers, "Buniadu, Gambia") sits over a full-width light-blue tinted band; centered tagline "Gemeinsam helfen • Nachhaltig unterstützen", bold category line "GESUNDHEIT • BILDUNG • GEMEINSCHAFT", two short intro paragraphs, one blue pill "unterstütze uns" CTA anchored to the donate section — all resolving within the first mobile screen.

FORM: Brief-pinned. The client supplied the complete direction as a Figma export (`figma_design_css`) fixing copy, section order, palette, and type scale across a 393px mobile frame and a 1280px desktop frame. No concept-seed roll was run — a pinned brief beats the roll, always. Photo assignment (client-approved 2026-09-10): rescue vehicle + founders + community group + clinic waiting room as a horizontally scrollable gallery inside "Unsere Geschichte"; man-with-children photo in "Schule"; pharmacy/dispensary photo in "Unser Personal". All three "unterstütze uns" buttons anchor to the donation section.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.

Unresolved decisions: real Impressum/Datenschutz legal text (placeholders shipped, flagged for replacement); real membership fee/registration details for the PDF form (placeholders shipped, flagged for replacement).
